# Prompt for Google Stitch (if connected)
Design a mobile-first web app called Jashn for planning weddings, birthdays and community events in India.
Home screen: the Event Graph workspace. A calendar-based graph where tasks are nodes positioned by date in lanes (planning, venue, food, decor, media,
entertainment, invites, logistics, event day). The critical chain is a bold continuous ribbon to an event-day marker; tasks with spare time have soft tails;
tasks with a backup vendor show a dashed ring. Top bar: event title, countdown, city chip, a readiness gauge (probability of being ready on time) and a resilience score.
Right panel (bottom sheet on mobile): node inspector with tabs Plan, Vendors, Backups, Risks. The Vendors tab shows a map above and ranked vendor cards below with
call, WhatsApp, directions, website and Set as backup. Also design: a template gallery with 12 illustrated cards, a one-screen intake sheet, a disruption drawer
showing ghost nodes of the old plan against the new plan, a what-if drawer with sliders, a budget donut with ledger, and a share sheet.
Calm, trustworthy, celebratory without cliche wedding-card ornament. Light and dark. Provide 3 variants of the workspace.
Constraints: 360px first, AA contrast, plain sentence-case copy, Hindi and Punjabi text must fit.
